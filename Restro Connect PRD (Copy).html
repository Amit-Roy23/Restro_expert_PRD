Restro Expert: Restaurant Business Management and Service Marketplace

Product Name Restro Expert
Document Date 3 October 2026
Primary Platforms Mobile (React Native) + Admin Web

Copyright © 2026 Aarohan Tech Solutions
One-Line Vision: "One integrated platform where a restaurant can hire verified people, source trusted vendors, and manage the money, attendance, and operations behind both."

1. Executive Summary

Restro Expert is a two-sided marketplace combined with a business-management toolkit purpose-built for the restaurant and food-service industry. It eliminates two severe everyday pain points for restaurant operators:

On-Demand Manpower

Chefs, cleaners, clerks, helpers, and waiters create profiles showcasing skills, experience, and their self-set asking prices.

Trusted Vendor Network

Direct discovery and quoting for commercial cleaning, food ingredients, and wholesale suppliers with reliable lead generation.

Operational Management

One unified console to manage attendance, calculate locked-rate payouts, track invoices, and monitor operational performance.

Monetization & Operations: The platform company operates the marketplace with an internal operations team (verification, attendance audits, payroll, follow-ups) and monetizes primarily through Premium Access Subscriptions for restaurant owners.

2. Problem Statement

Stakeholder Current Problem & Market Inefficiencies
Restaurant Owners Finding chefs, cleaners, and helpers relies on slow word-of-mouth or opaque agents. High no-show rates, unpredictable wage spikes, and no systematic record of who worked when.
Workers / Manpower Zero direct visibility to employers, no formal way to showcase verified skills and expected pay, frequent informal payment disputes and wage delays.
Vendors / Suppliers Dependent on fragmented personal connections to acquire restaurant clients with no predictable, steady inbound lead channel.
Platform Company Burdened with manual WhatsApp/phone follow-ups, disparate spreadsheets for attendance and payouts, and zero telemetry on user behavior.

3. Goals, Non-Goals & Success Metrics

3.1 Strategic Goals

1 Reduce the time to discover, request, and hire staff from days to hours.
2 Give workers an empowering, transparent profile with their own asking price and verified credentials.
3 Provide restaurant owners with an all-in-one console for worker attendance, payouts, and service billing.
4 Provide commercial food and cleaning vendors with a recurring lead pipeline from local restaurants.
5 Give the platform company complete operational oversight (internal staff, CRM follow-ups, user activity logs).
6 Create a sustainable, scalable recurring revenue stream via premium subscriptions and placement value.

3.2 Non-Goals (v1 MVP Scope Boundaries)

  • No Full E-Commerce Wholesale Cart: v1 relies on structured Quote & Service requests rather than live inventory cart checkout.
  • No Restaurant POS / Table Billing: "Billing" in v1 strictly represents platform service invoices, worker payouts, and vendor requests.
  • No In-App Statutory Payroll Compliance: Automated PF, ESI, and government TDS tax filings are out of scope for v1.
  • Android-First Focus: Initial release targets Android (React Native/Expo); iOS deployment scheduled for Phase 3.

7. Key End-to-End User Flows

The step-by-step operational workflows powering the core user journeys in Restro Expert:

1
Worker Onboarding & Verification: OTP Verification → Select "Worker" Role → Build Profile & Set Asking Rate → Upload Aadhaar/ID Document → Ops Staff Reviews & Approves → Status changes to Verified → Profile live in marketplace search.
2
Direct Manpower Hire (Happy Path): Owner filters "Chef, Kolkata, ₹1,200/day" → Views profile → Taps "Hire" with specified dates & shift → Worker gets push alert and taps "Accept" → Contract status becomes Active → Daily attendance tracked → Last day reached → Payout calculated → Owner approves & both parties submit star ratings.
3
Broadcast Open Staff Requirement: Owner posts "Need 3 Waiters for Saturday Dinner" → System matches local available waiters → Ops staff reviews & confirms candidates → Owner confirms selection → 3 Hire records automatically instantiated.
4
Vendor Quote & Service Delivery: Owner selects "Wholesale Food Supplies" → Submits item requirements list → Vendor responds with itemized quote → Owner accepts quote → Vendor delivers supplies → Digital invoice generated & marked settled.
5
Premium Subscription Upgrade: Owner encounters gated feature (e.g., direct contact credits) → Selects Quarterly Premium Plan → Pays via Razorpay → Premium badge instantly activated with auto-generated tax invoice.
6
Internal Operational CRM Follow-up: Invoice overdue > 48h triggers automated follow-up ticket → Assigned to Operations Executive → Staff member contacts restaurant owner → Records call outcome notes → Updates ticket to Resolved.
7
Dormant User Re-Engagement: Customer Activity Dashboard flags 14-day dormant restaurants → Operations team initiates assisted hiring outreach to drive retention.

10. Business Model & Money-Flow Decision

The platform supports four complementary revenue streams with three architectural money-flow models:

1. Premium Subscriptions

Recurring monthly/annual fee from restaurant owners for unlimited hires, priority placement, and advanced reports.

2. Marketplace Commission

Percentage or flat fee on confirmed worker shifts and vendor fulfillment orders.

3. Vendor Listing / Leads

Featured placement fees and qualified commercial lead package fees for suppliers.

4. Managed Staffing Fee

Value-added placement fee when company internal staff manually sources and matches workers.

Money-Flow Decision Matrix for Client

Model Option Operational Mechanism Strategic Pros Key Trade-offs
Model A: Direct Pay (Recommended for MVP) Restaurant owner pays worker directly in cash/UPI. Platform functions as the attendance & payout calculation ledger. Lowest regulatory & compliance friction, rapid time-to-market. Requires honor-system or subscription gating for monetization.
Model B: Platform Escrow Restaurant pays platform in advance. Platform settles to worker post-attendance deduction. Direct monetization capture, eliminates worker wage disputes. Higher banking integration and payment escrow overhead.
Model C: Agency of Record Company operates as the formal employer of record and invoices restaurants on corporate terms. Maximum revenue per placement, total operational lock-in. Heavy labor law compliance, PF/ESI liability, legal footprint.
Strategic Recommendation: Launch MVP with Model A (Ledger & Subscriptions) to establish network density quickly with minimal friction, transitioning to Model B (Integrated Escrow) in Phase 2.

13. Risk Assessment & Mitigations

Identified Risk Factor Impact Actionable Mitigation Strategy
Supply Cold-Start (Chicken & Egg) High Seed supply first: Pre-onboard and verify 100+ local workers and top vendors via ops team before onboarding restaurants.
Platform Bypass (Off-Platform Hires) High Mask contact numbers prior to hire acceptance; offer premium attendance, payout records, and verified guarantees that incentivize staying on-platform.
Worker No-Shows & Reliability High Two-way rating algorithms, strict no-show penalties, automated standby replacement candidate alerts.
Fraudulent Profiles & Credentials Medium Mandatory government ID document upload + human staff verification before "Verified" badge issuance.
Wage & Attendance Disputes Medium Locked agreement contracts, daily attendance sign-off, and internal staff dispute arbitration portal.
Low Digital Literacy Medium Ultra-simple large UI buttons, regional languages (Hindi, Bengali), and voice/staff-assisted onboarding.
